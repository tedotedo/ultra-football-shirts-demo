BNWT · DEADSTOCK

Brand New With Tags

Unworn collector or archive piece retaining original manufacturer swing tags. Crisp collar weave, untouched sponsor print, and storage-only handling.

9/10 MINT

Crisp Collector Condition

Vibrant colour saturation, firm ribbed cuffs/collar, and sharp crest/flock lettering with minimal to zero bobbling or pulls.

8/10 VERY GOOD

Clean Matchday Ready

Well-cared-for vintage piece showing light signs of age (e.g. minor wash-label fade or isolated micro-bobble) photographed and noted transparently on the card.

7/10 GOOD · TERRACE WORN

Honest Character Piece

Visible age-related wear such as sponsor hairline cracking or minor pulls — priced fairly for everyday matchday wear rather than framed display.
